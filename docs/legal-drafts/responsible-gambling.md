# Responsible Gambling (DRAFT)

DailyAI Betting is for entertainment and information only. We are not a sportsbook, we do not take bets, and nothing here is a guarantee of profit. Most people who bet lose money over time.

## If you choose to bet
- You must be 21 or older (or the legal age where you live, if higher). Betting is not legal in every state or country; know your local law.
- Only bet money you can afford to lose. Set deposit and loss limits before you start and stick to them.
- Never chase losses. A pick being popular ("consensus") does not make it likely to win.
- Treat picks, grades and AI articles as one input, not advice.
- Take breaks. Do not bet when stressed, angry, impaired, or to solve money problems.

## Warning signs
Betting more than planned, hiding it, borrowing to bet, feeling anxious or irritable when not betting, or neglecting work and relationships.

## Get help (US)
- National Problem Gambling Helpline: **1-800-GAMBLER** (1-800-426-2537), 24/7, free and confidential. Text and chat options at ncpgambling.org.
- Most licensed sportsbooks offer deposit limits, cool-off periods and self-exclusion in account settings; use them.

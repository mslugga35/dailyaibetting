# About DailyAI Betting (DRAFT)

DailyAI Betting shows where sports cappers agree. We collect picks from Discord-posted capper picks and public sites, normalize team names and bet types, group identical bets, and count how many cappers chose the same side ("consensus"). We then grade picks after games finish and publish the record.

## What we do
- Consensus picks by sport, with capper counts and tiers.
- Capper pages and a public leaderboard built from graded picks.
- A graded consensus record on the Results page. [[OWNER: describe grading method, units/ROI assumptions (estimated at -110), minimum sample and known gaps once the grading workstream finalizes]]
- A daily AI-written report (scheduled for about 10:30 AM ET; it may not publish every day) and AI-assisted blog articles.
- DailyAI Pro: all consensus picks, capper pick detail and the full pick history.

## What we are not
We are not a sportsbook and we do not guarantee outcomes. Consensus does not mean a pick will win. See Responsible Gambling.

This policy explains how cancelling and refunds work for DailyAI Pro subscriptions sold by

## 1. Plans and renewal
- DailyAI Pro is a monthly subscription. The price you pay is shown on the pricing page and on
  the Stripe checkout page before you pay, and on your Account page afterwards.
- **Founding Member price.** If you joined at the Founding Member price ($9.99/month), that
  price applies for as long as your subscription stays active. If the subscription ends
  (because you cancel and the paid period runs out, or because a payment cannot be collected
  after Stripe's retries), the founding price ends too; rejoining later is at the then-current
  regular price.
- Your subscription renews automatically each month at the same price until you cancel.

## 2. Free trial
- New members may receive a free trial (currently 7 days). One free trial per customer: the trial
  is for first-time members only, and a payment card that was already used for a DailyAI free trial
  cannot start another one: that new trial is cancelled right away, without any charge, and we email you.
- Your first charge happens when the trial ends; the date is shown before you start the trial.
  Cancel before the trial ends and you will not be charged.

## 3. How to cancel
- Sign in, open **Account**, choose **Manage Subscription**, then **Cancel** in the Stripe customer
  portal. You can also email [[SUPPORT EMAIL, e.g. support@dailyaibetting.com]] and we will cancel
  for you.
- Cancelling stops future charges. You keep Pro access until the end of the period you already
  paid for.

## 5. Failed payments
- If a renewal payment fails, Pro is paused and Stripe retries the charge. Update your card in

  subscription ends.
